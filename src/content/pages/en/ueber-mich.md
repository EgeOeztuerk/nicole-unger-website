I wrote my first short story at the age of seven, and at eight I stood on the school stage for the first time – in a French play, as a tree and as a whispering girl, for whom a special skirt was even sewn from coarse blue fabric.

My passion for singing and dancing led me to spend some time at musical theatre school after finishing school in 1991, before turning to classical theatre and, in particular, studying the work of Christoph Marthaler. My diploma thesis was devoted to the musical and aesthetic principles of his early productions, and it was an honour to interview some of his long-time companions for it.

Since 1995 I have acted, directed, devised and adapted plays for the stage – among them a four-actor version of Schiller's THE ROBBERS in Hamburg in 2000 with specially composed music, with Antonia von Romatowski in the female lead in one of her first roles. Her joy in playing, her humour and her immediacy now delight a wide comedy and TV audience. I also loved working with the Cologne theatre collective Futur3, whose participatory approach and socially relevant themes still inspire me.

With the improv theatre group Fenstersturz I was able to indulge my need for fun and spontaneity – the perfect balance to the text analysis and structural thinking that my work as an author and script reader requires.

In film and television I have had the honour of acting alongside renowned stars such as Barbara Sukowa, Vicky Krieps and Katja Riemann. Working with Margarethe von Trotta three times is among my most treasured experiences.
