As a graduate dramaturge (LMU Munich / Bavarian Theatre Academy August Everding) and long-standing industry insider (including development for Gate Filmproduktion and the evaluation of international film and TV projects for Senator, Universum, Telepool, Global Screen, RTL and Leonine), I know what makes stories work – in front of and behind the camera.

I help producers, distributors and writers unlock the full potential of their projects for the national and international market – precisely, with a clear sense of the market and a deep understanding of dramaturgy, and, on request, with attention to conscious storytelling.
