If you use the contact form, your details (name, email address, subject, message) are processed via Netlify's form service and forwarded to me in order to answer your enquiry.

## Videos and voice samples (Filmmakers)

Showreels and voice samples are embedded via the player of the Filmmakers platform (filmmakers.one GmbH, Berlin) using a two-click solution. A connection to filmmakers.eu is only established once you actively click "Play video" or "Load voice reel". Technically necessary data (e.g. your IP address and the page visited) is then transferred to Filmmakers. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR).
