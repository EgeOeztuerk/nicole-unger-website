## 2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by **Netlify, Inc.** (USA). Each time a page is requested, Netlify automatically processes data transmitted by your browser that is technically necessary to deliver the page: IP address, date and time of access, page requested, referrer URL, browser type and operating system. This data is not combined with other data and is not used to identify individuals.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is the secure and stable operation of the website. A data processing agreement is in place with Netlify. Transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on standard contractual clauses. More information: [netlify.com/privacy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## 3. Domain and email forwarding (Cloudflare)

The domain nicoleunger.com is managed via **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA). Cloudflare only handles name resolution (DNS); the website itself is not delivered through Cloudflare.

Emails to info@nicoleunger.com are forwarded to my personal mailbox via Cloudflare Email Routing. Cloudflare processes the sender address, recipient address and message content for this purpose without storing them permanently.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliable operation of the domain and a single contact address). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses. More information: [cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 4. Contact form

If you write to me via the contact form, I process your details (subject, name, email address, message) in order to answer your enquiry. The form is transmitted via Netlify's form service (see section 2) and stored there; I then receive a notification by email at info@nicoleunger.com (see section 3).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). I delete your data once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

## 5. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your email address and the information in your message in order to reply. Legal basis and retention period are the same as for the contact form (section 4).

## 6. Videos and voice samples (Filmmakers)

Showreels and voice samples are embedded via the player of the **Filmmakers** platform (filmmakers.one GmbH, Berlin) using a two-click solution: no connection whatsoever is made to Filmmakers until you click "Play video" or "Load voice reel".

Only after your click is the player loaded from filmmakers.eu. In particular, your IP address, the page visited and technical information about your browser are then transmitted to Filmmakers, and Filmmakers may set its own cookies. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, Section 25 (1) TDDDG). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by reloading the page and not activating the player again. More information: [filmmakers.eu – privacy](https://www.filmmakers.eu/en/privacy).

## 7. No cookies, no tracking, local fonts

This website itself sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services. Fonts are stored on this website's server; no connection is made to Google Fonts or any other font provider when a page is loaded.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to

- access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
- erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
- withdraw any consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

**Right to object:** Where I process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

A short message to info@nicoleunger.com is all it takes. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example in your place of residence.

## 9. Data security

This website is delivered exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections, so data you send via the contact form is transmitted in encrypted form.
